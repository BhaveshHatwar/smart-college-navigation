About the Project

Smart Indoor College Navigation System

Purpose

To provide students, faculty and visitors with an easy digital way to locate rooms, laboratories, offices and facilities inside the college building.

Features

  • Interactive floor maps
  • Four-floor navigation (C, D, E and F)
  • Search locations on every floor
  • Room information
  • Indoor route calculation
  • Stair and lift navigation
  • Responsive interface

How to use it

  • Pick a floor, or search for a room.
  • Tap a room to see its details.
  • Press Navigate Here and choose where you are.
  • Follow the orange route. It switches floors through the lift or stairs.

Technology

HTML, CSS and JavaScript on the front end. Firebase Authentication and Firestore on the back end. Maps are SVG. Routes are found with Dijkstra's shortest-path algorithm on a corridor graph.
